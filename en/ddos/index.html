Resilience against
DoS/DDoS

Advanced analysis of server and application operational capacity to determine performance limits, resource consumption, and resilience under overload scenarios.

Using controlled load testing and estimation models, we assess How many simultaneous users and requests an infrastructure can handleIdentifying the traffic volume at which performance begins to degrade.

Examining which services, processes, and operations consume the most CPU, memory, and bandwidth, detecting bottlenecks and critical points that could compromise system availability.

Based on the results, we develop projections of capacity and potential scenarios for denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks, estimating the impact of excessive loads, the likelihood of saturation under different scenarios, and the conditions necessary to maintain operational stability.

The goal is to anticipate the limits of the infrastructure, prevent disruptions, and optimize its capacity, providing performance statistics, technical estimates, and specialized recommendations to strengthen resilience
