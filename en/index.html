Our penetration testing subjects your systems to a rigorous evaluation.

After each evaluation, we provide a technical report with specific recommendations to correct the identified vulnerabilities, strengthen protective mechanisms, and reduce risks that could compromise the security of the infrastructure.

01

Web
applications.

We examine web application security through specialized penetration testing. We analyze authentication, access controls, sessions, and business logic, validating vulnerabilities such as SQLi, XSS, CSRF, SSRF, and IDOR using controlled exploitation techniques.

02

Servers &
cloud environments.

We conduct penetration testing on servers, networks, and cloud and hybrid environments. We assess exposed services and ports, CVE vulnerabilities, insecure configurations, IAM controls, and authentication mechanisms. We validate exploitation vectors, privilege escalation, and lateral movement to determine the technical scope of each vulnerability.

03

Internal &
external networks.

We measure external and internal exposure: ports, protocols, segmentation, firewalls, VPN and services such as SMB, RDP, DNS and SNMP.

04

API
security.

We verify authorization at the object and function level, BOLA/BFLA, IDOR, mass assignment, authentication, data exposure, injection vulnerabilities and rate limits.

05

Database
engines.

We conduct penetration testing on SQL and NoSQL engines, examining stored procedures, dynamic queries, data isolation, replication, and schema-level permissions. We verify scenarios for user access, execution of unexpected operations, and information exposure through controlled queries and transactions.

We apply intrusion tactics
used by cybercriminals.

We implement techniques used in real cyberattacks in controlled intrusion scenarios. The operations are designed to explore potential access routes, assess security mechanisms, and analyze how an organization responds to adversarial actions under authorized conditions.

Our own engineering

We develop LeensFish, our cybersecurity technology built on offensive research, international penetration-testing methodologies, and advanced analytical techniques.
It is designed to examine attack surfaces in depth, identify potential vulnerabilities, and investigate behaviours that conventional tools may overlook. LeensFish represents our applied-engineering approach to pentesting, combining in-house development, research, and specialised analysis.
